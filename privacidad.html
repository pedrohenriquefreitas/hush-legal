Hush — Privacy Policy

Effective: October 21, 2026 · Applies to Hush for macOS

The short version

Your audio and transcripts never leave your Mac. Hush performs all transcription, speaker labeling, and AI summarization on-device. Hush has no servers, no accounts, and collects no analytics.

What Hush processes, and where

  • Audio files and recordings you import or record are processed entirely on your Mac and stored in Hush's local library under your control.
  • Transcripts, summaries, and speaker profiles are stored locally. You can delete any or all of them at any time from within the app.
  • Model downloads: on first use, Hush downloads open-source speech models (e.g., Whisper) from public repositories. These downloads do not include any of your content.
  • Meeting recordings: Hush records system audio and your microphone only when you explicitly start a recording, and reminds you to obtain participants' consent. Recordings stay on your Mac.
  • Exports: if you choose to export or copy a transcript (for example, to share with an AI assistant), that copy leaves Hush's control — sharing is always your explicit action.

Data collection

Hush collects no data. No analytics, no crash-report content, no identifiers. The App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected."

Purchases

The optional Hush Pro one-time purchase is processed by Apple. Hush only receives an anonymous receipt confirming the purchase.
