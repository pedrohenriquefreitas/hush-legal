Hush — Support

Getting your WhatsApp voice notes in

  1. Open web.whatsapp.com in your browser.
  2. Click ⌄ on a voice message → Download.
  3. With Auto-Transcribe on, the transcript appears by itself. Otherwise drop the file on the Hush window.

Recording meetings

Menu bar → Record Meeting. The first time, macOS asks for System Audio Recording permission. Both sides of the call are transcribed and labeled You/Them — entirely on your Mac. Please make sure everyone consents to being recorded.

Common questions

Which languages? Automatic detection; English, Português, and Español are first-class, with ~100 languages supported by the Whisper models.

Why is the first transcription slow? Hush downloads its speech model once (hundreds of MB) and compiles it for the Neural Engine. After that, it's fast and fully offline.

Where are my files? Transcripts live in Hush's library and, if enabled, as Markdown/JSON in Downloads/Hush Transcripts for your AI assistant.
